Beyond Aesthetics — What ACTUALLY Matters When Buying a Phone Case

Design gets you to click "add to cart." But these factors determine whether you're still happy with your purchase three months later.

Protection Level

Every phone case should protect your phone. That's the baseline. But protection levels vary wildly, and understanding what you need prevents both overspending and under-protecting.

Light protection: Covers scratches and minor bumps. Suitable for careful users. Slim and clear cases typically fall here.

Medium protection: Handles drops from desk height (about 1 metre). Raised bezels keep screen and camera off surfaces. Most polycarbonate and good TPU cases deliver this level.

Heavy protection: Survives drops from pocket height and beyond. Reinforced corners, thicker profile, tested drop ratings. Tough cases are built for this. If you tend to drop your phone — and honesty here saves you money — go heavy.

Material and Finish

Material affects everything: hand feel, grip, durability, how the design looks, and how the case ages. Here's a quick breakdown.

Polycarbonate (PC): Hard, lightweight, holds prints well. The standard for quality phone cases. Doesn't yellow.

TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane): Flexible, shock-absorbing, great for bumpers. Can yellow over time in cheaper formulations.

Tempered Glass: Offers depth and luminosity to designs. Feels premium. Heavier. Best for glass-back cases where the design has rich detail.

Silicone: Soft-touch feel, good grip. Can attract lint and dust. Designs can stretch or distort over time.

Precise Fit

A case that doesn't fit your exact phone model perfectly is a waste of money. Buttons should click with precision. Port cutouts should be exact. The case should sit flush against the phone with no gaps or wobble. This sounds obvious, but an alarming number of girl phone cases from unverified sellers fail this basic test.

Buy from brands that design for specific models, not generic "fits most" cases. GRL® designs each case for each device individually — the cutouts, curves, and dimensions are model-specific. That precision is something you notice every time you press a button.
