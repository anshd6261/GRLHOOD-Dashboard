Caring for Your Aesthetic Phone Case — Making It Last

You've invested in a beautiful case. Here's how to keep it that way.

Regular Cleaning

Wipe your case with a soft, slightly damp microfiber cloth weekly. For tougher marks, use a tiny amount of mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals, alcohol-based cleaners, or abrasive materials — they can dull the finish and damage prints.

Avoid Prolonged Sun Exposure

Leaving your phone face-up in direct sunlight for hours can cause colour fading over time, even on high-quality cases. Keep it in shade when possible, especially during Indian summers where surface temperatures can get extreme.

Remove and Clean Underneath

Dust and debris can get trapped between your case and phone, causing micro-scratches on both surfaces. Pop your case off once a month and clean both the case interior and the phone back. It takes thirty seconds and prevents long-term damage.
